Aurum

The weight of excellence

Forged from 18-karat rose gold, Aurum represents the pinnacle of horological artistry. Each piece is assembled by a single master watchmaker over 340 hours, housing the proprietary Calibre A1 movement — a symphony of 287 components beating at 28,800 vibrations per hour.

The domed sapphire crystal reveals a hand-guilloché dial in champagne soleil, where applied gold indices catch light like a sunset frozen in time. The alligator leather strap, hand-stitched in Italy, completes a presence that commands attention without demanding it.

Lumen

Light, refined

Lumen reimagines the relationship between space and illumination. A solid billet of aircraft-grade aluminum, CNC-machined to 0.02mm tolerance, houses a custom-engineered COB LED module that renders color with 95+ CRI fidelity — indistinguishable from natural daylight.

The borosilicate glass diffuser, mouth-blown in Germany, shapes light into a gradient so seamless it appears to float. Touch-sensitive controls on the tapered stem offer 1000 levels of dimming, while tunable white shifts from warm candlelight to crisp midday. No app. No hub. Just light.
